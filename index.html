the studio

Meet Sanaa — one brush, one pair of scissors, a lot of colour

Sanaa started Bloom & Brush on a kitchen table with a stack of plain tees and a bunch of leftover market flowers. It's still that personal — every order is painted, cut, or wrapped by her hand, in small batches, so nothing ever looks quite like anything else.

No factories, no printers. Just a table full of paint tubes and buckets of whatever's in season this week.

"If it doesn't make me want to keep it for myself, it doesn't leave the studio."
— Sanaa artist & founder, Bloom & Brush
